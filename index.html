03Why

Evidence, not summaries.

In private capital, being wrong is expensive. Being slow is fatal.

Traditional diligence is rigorous but slow. AI tools are fast because they summarize — confident prose with nothing underneath.

We run commercial and financial diligence as one exercise, every number traced to its source. The financials say what happened; the commercial evidence — market, customers, channels, supply — says whether it holds. That is decision-grade.

AI does the compiling. Credit analysts own every judgment.
