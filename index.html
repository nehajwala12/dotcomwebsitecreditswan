Three claims from an invented management case. Pick your ruling on each, then see what the evidence said.

0 of 3 called

Claim 1 · Project Fern

“The decline sits in a few sites. Close them and it is cured.”

Our ruling: Partly supported, leaning supported

Nine of 57 stores explain most of the decline. But same-site visits fell 6.5% a year while peers held flat, and the 48 sites that stay carry most of that fall.

Claim 2 · Project Fern

“Comparable sales are flat.”

Our ruling: Contradicted

On the company’s own site list they are. Add back the six units it closed and comparable sales fall a further full point.

Claim 3 · Project Fern

“The first-year plan is achievable.”

Our ruling: Not enough evidence yet

Management hit its first-year plan in two of six years, and 44% of annual EBITDA lands in twelve weeks that the plan spreads evenly. We asked for a monthly plan by site.
